Overview

The Orchard-9 irrigation controller regulates water delivery across up to sixteen independent zones. The unit continuously samples soil-moisture telemetry from each zone and modulates valve duty-cycles to hold the configured moisture set-point within a two-percent tolerance band, compensating for evaporation, rainfall, and line-pressure variance in real time.

Before first use, confirm the mains supply is within the rated 90–264 VAC window and that the zone manifold is fully primed. Operating the unit against an unprimed manifold can trip the dry-run cutout.

Caution
Do not connect zones 13–16 while the auxiliary expansion board is absent. The controller will report a phantom fault and lock the affected zones until power-cycled.

Zone configuration

Each zone carries an independent schedule, a moisture set-point, and a maximum daily volume cap. Schedules may overlap; the controller arbitrates contention by line-pressure headroom, deferring lower-priority zones until sufficient pressure is available. A zone left unconfigured defaults to manual-only and will never actuate on a schedule.

Telemetry and faults

The controller retains a rolling seventy-two-hour telemetry buffer. When a sensor reads outside its plausibility envelope for more than three consecutive samples, the affected zone is flagged degraded and falls back to its last known-good duty-cycle rather than actuating on suspect data. Persistent degradation raises a maintenance flag on the status page.

  • Green — zone nominal, actuating on schedule.
  • Amber — zone degraded, holding last known-good duty-cycle.
  • Red — zone faulted, actuation suspended pending acknowledgement.

Maintenance

Flush the manifold quarterly and replace the inline filter cartridge whenever the differential-pressure indicator enters the amber band. Record each service against the unit serial in the maintenance log so warranty coverage is preserved.
